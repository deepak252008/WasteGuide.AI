# Define Problem Statements

## 1. Problem Definition
Urban centers in India, such as Hyderabad, generate thousands of metric tons of municipal waste daily. A significant portion of this waste is mixed at source due to household segregation confusion, leading to:
- Excessive pressure on public landfills.
- Hazardous substances (electronics, batteries, medicines) leaching into water systems.
- Recyclable materials (plastics, aluminum, glass) being contaminated and made unrecyclable.

---

## 2. Core Problem Statements

### Problem Statement 1: Source Segregation Confusion
> **How might we** assist residents in instantly determining the precise recycling protocol, category, and hazards of common household items at the exact moment of disposal?

### Problem Statement 2: Specialized Disposal Facility Accessibility
> **How might we** direct residents to regional drop-off centers that accept specialized materials (like E-waste or Hazardous waste) without demanding expensive navigation subscriptions or paid maps?

### Problem Statement 3: Lack of Habit Tracking
> **How might we** provide users with clear visual feedback on their recycling habits to motivate daily separation and build long-term green habits?
