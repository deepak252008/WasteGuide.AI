# Customer Journey Map

This journey maps the experience of **Karthik**, a resident of Jubilee Hills, Hyderabad, trying to dispose of an old laptop battery:

```
[Phase 1: Need] ──> [Phase 2: Search] ──> [Phase 3: Decision] ──> [Phase 4: Action] ──> [Phase 5: Habits]
```

### 1. Phase 1: Awareness of Need
- **User Action**: Karthik finds an old, swollen lithium battery in his drawer.
- **Thinking**: "I can't throw this in the regular dustbin. It might catch fire."
- **Feeling**: Anxious, cautious.

### 2. Phase 2: Search and Scan
- **User Action**: Karthik opens WasteGuide AI, types "Battery" (or clicks the quick chip), and clicks SCAN.
- **Thinking**: "Hope this tells me where to go."
- **Feeling**: Anticipant.

### 3. Phase 3: Decision / Interpretation
- **User Action**: App displays a red **Hazard Warning** ("Contains heavy metals... Can cause fire if terminals short"), 4 recycling steps, and classifies it as **Hazardous Waste**.
- **Thinking**: "I definitely need to take this to a specialized center."
- **Feeling**: Informed and responsible.

### 4. Phase 4: Action / Navigation
- **User Action**: Karthik clicks "FIND HYDERABAD COLLECTION CENTERS". The map filters for hazardous waste depots, showing *Ramky Hazardous Waste Facility* at Patancheru, and lists its contact number and distance.
- **Thinking**: "I will drop it off there this weekend."
- **Feeling**: Relieved.

### 5. Phase 5: Feedback / Habit Building
- **User Action**: Karthik checks his Dashboard page, seeing his Recyclable count increase and his habit charts update.
- **Thinking**: "It's rewarding to see my scan statistics grow. I'll segregate my plastics next."
- **Feeling**: Proud and motivated.
